## Message from the dev

After mentioning that Firestone got pirated, I got an overwhelming number of support message from the community - especially people from China, some of them even offering me to help make Firestone better. That was heartwarming, and I was moved by such support.

The initial author of the hack also contacted me, apologized and took down their server. I must admit that I didn't expect that, and I have a deep respect for this kind of very humane and humble behiavor. Again, thank you from the bottom of my heart.

I want to thank once again all the users who use Firestone, and especially the subscribers from all over the world. It's thanks to you that Firestone exists.
