## Important - Message from the dev

I am aware that programs that grant you Firestone membership for free have started circulating recently, especially on the China server.

I have been building Firestone on my own for seven years, and put tens of thousands of hour of work into it. I am a solo developer, and rely fully on the income generated by the app.  
At the same time, I try to offer a lot of things for free - you can use almost all features every day without becoming a premium user.

If you use and enjoy Firestone, please consider all of this before pirating the app. Firestone offers everything behind a single subscription - you don't need to buy different memberships for each game mode. Firestone also offers 6-month and 12-month subscriptions to make the membership even cheaper.

If you're already a subscriber - thank you. It's thanks to you that I can continue doing what I love and offer the app to everyone for free.  
If you're a free user - thank you too. Firestone wouldn't be what it is today without its community.  
If you pirate the app today - please reconsider.

Thanks for reading me :)
