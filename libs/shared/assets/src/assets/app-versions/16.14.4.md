## Replays

- ✨ (PREMIUM) In-game replays are finally there!
    - Starting from now, all games you play can be reviewed as if you were spectating a Hearthstone game, using the game's engine. This mean you get all the animations and sound effects in your replay.
    - You get additional controls to change the replay speed, and can pause the replay at anytime
    - Traditional Hearthstone (Constructed, Arena, etc.) is supported, as well as Battlegrounds
    - To use it, you will first need to activate the Hearthstone Replay Viewer mod in the settings
        - Unfortunately, this means that his feature won't be available on the mainland China server, as NetEase has banned all mods
    - This is still an early version, so please let me know (with the Discord link at the top of the app) if there are
